## How I approach engineering

- **Start with the business rule.** Clarify the workflow and source of truth before choosing an implementation.
- **Design for uncertainty.** A cross-system operation can succeed, fail, or leave an unknown outcome; recovery must respect the cost of a wrong assumption.
- **Keep AI connected to evidence.** Preserve source references and use executable queries where precision matters.
- **Separate reasoning from execution.** Let models interpret and propose; enforce critical operations through validated contracts and APIs.
- **Make knowledge reusable.** Encode recurring analysis into schemas, documentation, and skills.
- **Check assumptions against real usage.** Let operational evidence change the design.
